TV delay

Holds back everything during a live game (scores, clock, plays) so the board doesn't spoil what's about to happen on your TV. Each sport's broadcast runs at its own delay. Start at 0; if the board is ahead of the TV, add seconds for that sport.

Photos

Put a photo on the board for a while (1 minute to 24 hours), mixed into the slides or on its own; then it's permanently wiped from the board. The photo page is simple on purpose: open it on a phone and use Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.

Its address, http://(board address):8080, opens only the photo page, never these settings, so it's safe to give to guests. It works on every phone and computer on your home WiFi.

Private league? Add your ESPN login codes

First, add your league ID above. If your league name and teams show up, it's public and you're done. Only if it says private, the board needs two codes from your ESPN login. You need a computer (Chrome or Edge) for this, about 2 minutes, once a year at most. They stay on the board and never show here again, and with them the board finds your team by itself.

  1. On the computer, open this settings page too (so you can copy and paste across).
  2. In another tab, go to espn.com/fantasy/football and sign in.
  3. Press F12. A panel of developer tools opens (side or bottom).
  4. Along the top of that panel, click Application (not there? click » to find it).
  5. On the left, under Storage, click the arrow next to Cookies, then https://www.espn.com.
  6. In the Filter box above the table, type espn_s2.
  7. Double-click the long text in the Value column, press Ctrl+C, and paste it into the espn_s2 box below.
  8. Change the filter to SWID and copy its value the same way. It looks like {1A2B3C4D-…}: include the curly brackets. Paste it into the SWID box.
  9. Tap Save codes, press F12 to close the panel, and reload this page: your league shows My team (…).

Value won't select? Click the row once: the full value shows at the bottom of the panel to copy. If ESPN later stops accepting the codes (about once a year), the league will say so: just do these steps again.

Yahoo app codes (one-time setup)

Yahoo only lets the board in through a registered app. Create one at developer.yahoo.com/apps/create (Fantasy Sports: Read, redirect URI https://localhost:8080), then paste its Client ID and Client Secret here. They stay on the board and never show here again.

Division rivals

Also follow the other teams in your favorites' divisions: their last and next games show after the standings. Their live games can show too, kept low-key (scores only, no celebrations).

Show it off

Play these on the board anytime. They also play by themselves: the startup show at power-up, the others after a Cubs win, a Blackhawks goal or a Bears win.

Smart lights

Flash the lights around the TV in team colors with the board's big moments (they match your TV delay), then put them back how they were. Works with Philips Hue, Govee, WiZ, LIFX, Nanoleaf and WLED lights on the same WiFi. Govee: first turn on LAN Control for each light in the Govee Home app.
